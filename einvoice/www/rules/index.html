The row gives you the answer most lookups actually need — engine severity, profile, the BT/BG business terms the rule touches and the XML location it points at. 297 rules in total — 284 fatal, 12 warning, 1 information — across 21 families. Family headings are the standard EN 16931 / XRechnung rule-family labels; every cell below is rendered from the remediation catalog the engine itself validates against. Back to the overview.

Profile says which artifact asserts the rule: en16931 for the 198 rules that come from the EN 16931 UBL Schematron (they fire for any EN 16931 invoice), xrechnung for the 99 that come from the German KoSIT XRechnung Schematron (they fire only when the invoice claims an XRechnung customization). Severity is what the engine does: fatal fails the run, warning and information are reported without failing it unless you raise the threshold. XML location is the element the rule is anchored to, written as the UBL/CII path fragment the report prints.
